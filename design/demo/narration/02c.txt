The trade page. The chart is the session so far, the strike a dashed line, a crosshair to read any moment. The ladder shows Yes and No prices, the implied chance, and what a dollar pays if you're right. The order book shows depth and flips to the No side, because No is one dollar minus Yes: one book, two ways to read it. Change the quantity and the panel tells you what you'd pay and win before you sign anything.
