The trade page. The chart is the session so far, with the strike as a dashed line, and a crosshair to read any point. Below it, the ladder: for every strike you see the Yes and No prices, the implied chance, and what a dollar pays if you're right. Pick a strike and the panel follows. The order book shows the depth on either side, and you can flip it to see the same book from the No perspective, because No is simply one dollar minus Yes. Change the quantity and the panel tells you exactly what you'd pay and what you'd win, before you sign anything.
