A market maker deposits one dollar per pair and mints a Yes and a No token. Those dollars sit in the market's vault on chain, not with us. The maker then quotes both sides: offering to sell Yes at fifty-five cents, and to buy Yes at forty-five. Every quote is an on-chain order on a single book, so the No side is just one minus the Yes price. Each action is one wallet signature, and the app confirms it as soon as the transaction lands.
