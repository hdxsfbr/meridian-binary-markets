A maker deposits one dollar per pair and mints a Yes and a No token. The dollars go into the market's vault, a program-owned account, not anyone's custody. With the pair, the maker quotes both sides: selling Yes at fifty-five cents, buying it back at forty-five. The quotes are queued and submitted together, each a real order on the on-chain book, one signature per action, and a confirmation when it lands. The maker earns the spread and carries the risk of whichever side the market takes.
