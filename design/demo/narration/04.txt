A maker deposits one dollar per pair and mints a Yes and a No token. Those dollars go into the market's vault, a program-owned account on chain, not into anyone's custody. With the pair in hand, the maker quotes both sides: offering to sell Yes at fifty-five cents, and to buy Yes at forty-five. The quotes are queued and submitted together, each one a real on-chain order on a single book. One wallet signature per action, and a confirmation the moment the transaction lands. The maker's edge is the spread; the risk is whichever side the market takes.
