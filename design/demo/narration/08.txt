At the close, trading stops on chain; the program refuses any order after that second. The settlement service fetches the closing price from the Pyth oracle, posts it to Solana, and the program checks it: fresh, tight confidence, published at the close. Then it settles every market against that one price. This one closed below the strike, so No wins. Winners redeem at a dollar per token: the maker collects ten dollars with a single signature, and the balance updates on the spot. Losing tokens are worth zero and can be cleared from the wallet. No custodian, no margin, no counterparty: the vault pays the winning side, and nothing else.
