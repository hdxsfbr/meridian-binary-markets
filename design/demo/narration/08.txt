At the close, trading stops on chain. The settlement service fetches the closing price from the Pyth oracle, posts it to Solana, and the program settles every market against it. This one closed below the strike, so No wins. Winners redeem at a dollar per token: the maker collects ten dollars with one signature, and the balance updates. Losers' tokens are simply worth zero. No custody, no margin, no counterparty: the vault pays the winning side and nothing else.
