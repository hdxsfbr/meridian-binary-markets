Most people have an opinion about where a stock will close today. Options let them act on it, with margin, Greeks, and unlimited downside. Meridian strips that to one question: will a stock close at or above a price today? Each market has two tokens, Yes and No, and together they always pay exactly one dollar. Buy the side you believe for less than a dollar; right, it's worth a dollar at the close; wrong, it's worth nothing. Known gain, known loss, no leverage. The headline cycles through the listed assets with a live chart, and the strike comes from the price right now.
