Meridian is a market for one simple question: will a stock close at or above a price today? Each market has two tokens, Yes and No, and together they always pay exactly one dollar. You buy the side you believe for less than a dollar. If you're right, it's worth a dollar at the close; if not, it's worth nothing. Known gain, known loss, no leverage, no margin calls. The headline cycles through the listed assets with a live chart of the day so far, and the strike you see is taken from the price right now.
